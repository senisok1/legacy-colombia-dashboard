## 1. FAQ Page — Final Copy

### Booking & Payment

**How do I book, and when do I pay?**
Full payment (100%) is due at the time of booking to confirm your reservation. There's a $250 separate deposit to be paid after booking.

**What's your cancellation policy?**
- Cancel 365+ days before arrival: 95% refund
- Cancel 120+ days before arrival: 50% refund
- Cancel within 120 days of arrival: no refund

We recommend travel insurance for flexibility closer to your trip.

**Is there a security deposit?**
There's a $250 separate deposit to be paid after booking.

**Why book directly instead of through Airbnb or Vrbo?**
Booking direct means no platform service fees — guests typically save around 15% compared to booking the same stay through a third-party platform, at the same great villa.

### Stay Details

**What are check-in/check-out times?**
Check-in is 3:00 PM, check-out is 11:00 AM.

**What's the minimum stay?**
1 night minimum (may be longer during peak season — happy to confirm for your specific dates).

**How many guests can the villa sleep?**
The villa comfortably sleeps up to 18 guests across 4 large en-suite bedrooms (2 master suites), with multiple lakefront rooms and water views.

**Is there a minimum age to book?**
Yes, the primary guest/renter must be at least 25 years old.

### Policies

**Are pets allowed?**
Yes — up to 2 pets are welcome with advance notice. Please reach out ahead of time so we can prepare. A pet fee will be charged.

**Are events or parties allowed?**
Events and parties are not permitted without prior approval, and any approved event may require on-site management supervision. Quiet hours are 11:00 PM–7:00 AM out of respect for neighbors.

**Is smoking allowed?**
No, the property is non-smoking.

**Is there security/surveillance on the property?**
Yes, there is gated security with 24-hour security guards. Also for guest safety there are exterior security cameras on the property (none inside living spaces).

**Do you require ID/passport registration?**
Yes — before check-in, all guests are sent a link to register with their passport, as required by local regulation.

**Is there an additional tax for Colombian residents?**
Yes — Colombian tax law requires an additional 19% tax charge on the reservation cost for guests who are Colombian residents. You'll be contacted after booking if this applies.

### Amenities

**What wellness amenities are included?**
Private jacuzzi, barrel sauna with cold-plunge/ice bath option, state-of-the-art gym, outdoor fitness playground, private tennis court, private dock with kayaking access, and over 1,500 feet of waterfront. An optional professionally-chilled cold plunge experience is available for $95/stay with 24-hour advance notice.

**Is WiFi available?**
Yes, Starlink high-speed WiFi covers the entire property — details are provided in your check-in guide once booked.
