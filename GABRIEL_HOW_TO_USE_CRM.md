# How to Use the CRM — For Gabriel

## 1. How to log in

1. Open your phone or computer browser (like Chrome or Safari).
2. Go to this website: **https://crm.legacyestaterentals.com**
3. Type in your email and password.
4. Tap or click the "Log In" button.

That's it! You're in.

The website will already show up in **your language** (Spanish) automatically once you log in. You don't need to change any settings — Seni set that up for you. If it ever shows in English by mistake, message Seni and he can fix it in one click. You can also toggle between USD and COP in the top right.

## 2. What you'll see when you log in

At the top of the screen, there's a menu bar. As a team member, you'll mainly use these tabs:

- **Dashboard** — a quick snapshot of guests checking in and out.
- **Management** — the list of stays (bookings), guest info, and your work log.
- **Commissions** — this is YOUR money tab. More on this below.
- **Team Log** — where you write notes about what you did each day.
- **Team Expenses** — where you ask to be paid back for something you bought for a property.
- **Settings** — where you can change your own password.

## 3. The Commissions tab (your money)

This is the most important tab for you. It shows:

- **"Owed to Gabriel"** — the total money you're owed (approved and locked in).
- **"Awaiting approval"** — money that's still waiting for Seni to approve.

### Logging an "extra"

If you arrange something extra for a guest (like a driver, a tour, or extra cleaning) you MUST add it here:

1. Tap **"+ Log an extra"**.
2. Pick the guest's stay from the list.
3. Pick what kind of extra it was.
4. Type in how much the guest paid, and how much the vendor was paid.
5. Tap **Save**.

The app automatically splits the total commission difference 50/50 between you and Seni — you'll see both amounts right away.

### Direct bookings you bring in

If you book a guest directly (not through the usual booking sites), the app automatically finds it and gives you 10% commission. You don't have to do anything — it shows up in your Commissions tab by itself.

### Getting paid

When Seni comes to visit and pays you in cash (COP), he'll tap "Settled" on your commissions. That doesn't erase anything — it keeps a permanent record of every payment, with the exchange rate used, so you can always look back and see exactly what you were paid and when.

## 4. A few simple rules

- You can **see** all your commission numbers any time, but you **cannot edit or delete** an entry once Seni approves or settles it. This protects both of you — there's always an honest record.
- If something looks wrong, message Seni — don't try to fix it yourself in the app.
- Your login only shows you Legacy Colombia. You won't see other properties, and that's normal.

## 5. If something doesn't work

Message Seni directly. Include:
- What page you were on
- What you tapped
- A screenshot if you can

That's everything you need to get started. Welcome to the CRM!
