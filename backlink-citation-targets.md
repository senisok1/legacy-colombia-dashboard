## Tier 1 — Villa rental directories (free, commission-only, high relevance)

These are legitimate luxury villa marketplaces. Listing is free; they only
take a cut when a booking comes through them — so worst case is zero cost,
best case is a real backlink plus incremental bookings.

1. **Villas of Distinction** — [villasofdistinction.com/list-your-villa](https://www.villasofdistinction.com/list-your-villa). Self-serve submission form; their Villa Product Team reviews for fit before listing.
2. **Top Villas** — [thetopvillas.com/en_us/list-your-vacation-home](https://www.thetopvillas.com/en_us/list-your-vacation-home). Free listing, you keep control of your calendar; they also push distribution to Google Travel, Airbnb, Vrbo, Expedia.
3. **Exceptional Villas** — [exceptionalvillas.com](https://www.exceptionalvillas.com/) — 3,700+ villa portfolio, no self-serve form found; needs a direct email pitch (contact page on site).
4. **Isle Blue** — [isleblue.co](https://isleblue.co/) — curated Caribbean/Latin America focus, good geographic fit; contact-page pitch, no self-serve form.

Skipped: Onefinestay, Relais & Châteaux, Four Seasons Villa & Residence Rentals — all require an invite/vetting process well above a single independent villa's profile, not worth the time investment right now.
